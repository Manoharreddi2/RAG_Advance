# Employee Handbook

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Helix Systems Inc. Helix was founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas, and builds database and analytics software for enterprises. Today the company has about 1,400 employees in offices in Austin, Dublin and Bangalore. This handbook explains the policies, benefits and expectations that apply to all employees.

## 2. Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours. Core collaboration hours are from 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone, during which employees are expected to be available for meetings. Outside core hours, employees may organize their working time flexibly with their manager's agreement.

Overtime for non-exempt employees must be approved in advance by the manager and is paid at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Paid Time Off

### 3.1 Vacation

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. After 5 years of service, the allowance increases to 25 days. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance in the PeopleHub system.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days.

### 3.3 Parental Leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents receive 10 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave can be taken at any time within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

### 3.4 Public Holidays

Each office observes the public holidays of its country. In addition, every employee receives one floating holiday per year that can be used for a cultural or religious holiday of their choice.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Benefits

### 4.1 Health Insurance

The company pays 90% of the health insurance premium for employees and 75% for their dependents. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.

### 4.2 Retirement Plan

In the United States, Helix matches 100% of employee contributions to the 401(k) plan up to 4% of salary, and 50% of the next 2%. Matching contributions vest immediately.

### 4.3 Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

### 4.4 Equipment

New employees receive a laptop of their choice from the approved hardware list and a one-time home office allowance of 500 USD.

## 5. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from at least two peers and an assessment by the manager. Salary adjustments are decided once a year after the December review and take effect on February 1.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 6. Code of Conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Concerns can be reported to HR, to any manager, or anonymously through the EthicsLine hotline, which is operated by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day.

Gifts from customers or vendors with a value above 100 USD must be declined or reported to the compliance team.

## 7. Leaving the Company

Employees who resign are asked to give a notice period of 4 weeks. Managers and senior staff are asked to give 8 weeks of notice. Company equipment must be returned on or before the last working day.
