# Q3 2024 Financial Report

## Executive Summary

Helix Systems Inc. reported total revenue of 182.4 million USD for the third quarter of 2024, an increase of 14% compared with the third quarter of 2023. Growth was driven by the cloud database product HelixDB Cloud, whose revenue grew 31% year over year. Operating margin improved to 18.2% from 15.6% a year earlier.

The company ended the quarter with 412 million USD in cash and short-term investments and no debt.

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Q3 2024 revenue (USD millions) | Q3 2023 revenue (USD millions) | Year-over-year growth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HelixDB Cloud | 96.1 | 73.4 | 31% |
| HelixDB Enterprise (on-premises) | 51.7 | 55.2 | -6% |
| Analytics Suite | 24.9 | 22.1 | 13% |
| Professional Services | 9.7 | 9.3 | 4% |
| Total | 182.4 | 160.0 | 14% |

On-premises license revenue declined as more customers migrated to the cloud offering.

## Customers

The number of customers with annual recurring revenue above 100,000 USD reached 687, up from 590 a year earlier. Net revenue retention was 118%. Gross customer churn was 4.1% on an annualized basis.

## Operating Expenses

Research and development expenses were 46.3 million USD, or 25% of revenue. Sales and marketing expenses were 52.9 million USD. General and administrative expenses were 16.8 million USD. Headcount at the end of the quarter was 1,412 employees.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Outlook

For the fourth quarter of 2024, the company expects revenue between 190 million and 194 million USD. For the full year 2024, the company now expects revenue between 712 million and 716 million USD, raised from the previous guidance of 700 million to 708 million USD.

## Risks

Key risks include increased competition from hyperscale cloud providers, foreign exchange fluctuations affecting European revenue, and the timing of large enterprise renewals. These results are unaudited.
