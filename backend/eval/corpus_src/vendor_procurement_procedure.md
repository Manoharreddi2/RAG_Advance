# Vendor Procurement Procedure

## 1. Scope

This procedure applies to all purchases of goods and services from external vendors by Helix Systems Inc., including software subscriptions, hardware, consulting and marketing services.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

Every purchase requires a purchase request in the ProcureFlow system before an order is placed. The required approvals depend on the total contract value.

| Total contract value (USD) | Required approval | Competitive quotes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 10,000 | Budget owner | Not required |
| 10,001 to 50,000 | Budget owner and department director | 2 quotes |
| 50,001 to 250,000 | Department VP and finance controller | 3 quotes |
| Above 250,000 | CFO and CEO | Formal request for proposal (RFP) |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Vendor Onboarding

New vendors must be registered in ProcureFlow before the first payment. Registration requires a completed W-9 or equivalent tax form, bank details verified by a call-back to a known phone number, and confirmation that the vendor is not on any sanctions list.

Vendors that will process customer data or Confidential information must also pass a security assessment by the security team before a contract is signed. The security assessment is valid for 2 years.

## 4. Contracts

Contracts above 50,000 USD must be reviewed by the legal team. Standard payment terms are net 45 days from the invoice date. Auto-renewal clauses must be flagged in ProcureFlow so that the budget owner receives a reminder 90 days before renewal.

## 5. Preferred Vendors

Purchases from vendors on the preferred vendor list do not require competitive quotes for contracts up to 50,000 USD, because pricing has already been negotiated. The preferred vendor list is reviewed every year in October.

## 6. Conflicts of Interest

Employees involved in selecting a vendor must disclose any personal or financial relationship with that vendor to the compliance team before the selection process starts.
