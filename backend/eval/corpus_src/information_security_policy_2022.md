# Information Security Policy v3

## 1. Introduction

This Information Security Policy (version 3) sets the minimum security requirements for all Helix Systems Inc. employees, contractors and systems. It was approved by the Chief Information Security Officer on June 10, 2022. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 2. Data Classification

All company information must be classified into one of three levels: Public, Internal and Confidential. Confidential information includes customer data, source code, financial results before publication and employee personal data. Confidential information must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## 3. Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must contain upper-case letters, lower-case letters and digits. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for VPN access and for the email system. SMS codes are an accepted second factor.

User accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and must lock the screen automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may be used to read company email only if they are enrolled in the mobile device management system.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## 5. Access Management

Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis and must be approved by the system owner. Access rights are reviewed once a year. When an employee leaves the company, all access must be revoked within 3 business days.

## 6. Security Training

All employees must complete security awareness training when they join and then once every year. Phishing simulations are carried out twice a year.

## 7. Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@helixsystems.example as soon as possible and no later than 24 hours after discovery.
