# Travel and Expense Policy 2023

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how employees of Helix Systems Inc. book business travel and claim reimbursement for business expenses. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees, interns and contractors who travel on company business. The policy is effective from February 1, 2023 and replaces all earlier travel guidance.

Expenses must be reasonable, necessary and directly related to company business. Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as they would spend their own.

## 2. Booking Travel

All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the corporate travel portal, TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are reimbursed only if the employee can show that the fare was at least 15% cheaper than the TripDesk fare for the same itinerary.

Travel must be approved by the employee's direct manager before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from a department vice president at least 21 days before departure.

### 2.1 Air Travel

Employees must fly economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is permitted only for flights longer than 10 hours and only with vice president approval.

Checked baggage fees are reimbursable for one bag on trips of up to 5 days and for two bags on longer trips.

<!-- pagebreak -->

### 2.2 Hotels

Employees should stay at hotels in the TripDesk preferred list where available. The nightly room rate, excluding taxes, must not exceed the city cap below.

| City tier | Example cities | Nightly cap (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | New York, San Francisco, London | 275 |
| Tier 2 | Chicago, Boston, Berlin | 210 |
| Tier 3 | All other cities | 160 |

Room upgrades, minibar charges and in-room movies are not reimbursable.

### 2.3 Ground Transportation

Taxis and ride-share services are reimbursable for travel between airports, hotels and business meetings. Rental cars are allowed when they are cheaper than taxis or when public transport is not available. Employees must decline the rental company's collision damage waiver because the company's insurance already covers rental cars.

Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at 58 cents per mile, which covers fuel, insurance and wear.

## 3. Meals and Per Diem

Employees traveling overnight receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) instead of submitting meal receipts. The per diem for domestic travel is 65 USD per day. On the first and last day of travel, 75% of the per diem is paid.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a director.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Submitting Expense Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the ExpenseHub system within 45 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than 45 days require written approval from the finance controller and may be rejected.

Itemized receipts are required for every expense of 30 USD or more. For expenses under 30 USD, a description of the expense in ExpenseHub is sufficient.

Approved claims are reimbursed by direct deposit within 10 business days.

### 4.1 Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year are eligible for a corporate card. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited. Charges on the corporate card must still be reconciled in ExpenseHub within 45 days.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are never reimbursed: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, gym or spa fees, lost luggage replacement beyond the airline's compensation, and travel for spouses or family members.

This policy is owned by the Finance department. Questions should be sent to travel@helixsystems.example.
