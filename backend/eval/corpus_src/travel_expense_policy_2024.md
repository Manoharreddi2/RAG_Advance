# Travel and Expense Policy 2024

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how employees of Helix Systems Inc. book business travel and claim reimbursement for business expenses. It applies to all employees, interns and contractors who travel on company business. The policy is effective from March 15, 2024 and supersedes the Travel and Expense Policy 2023.

Summary of the main changes in 2024: higher hotel caps, a new sustainability rule for short trips, a shorter claim deadline and an increased mileage rate.

## 2. Booking Travel

All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the corporate travel portal, TripDesk. Bookings made outside TripDesk are not reimbursed, except in a documented emergency.

Travel must be approved by the employee's direct manager before booking. International travel additionally requires approval from a department vice president at least 14 days before departure.

### 2.1 Air Travel

Employees must fly economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is permitted for flights longer than 9 hours with vice president approval.

Sustainability rule: for trips where the train journey takes less than 4 hours, employees must take the train instead of flying.

Checked baggage fees are reimbursable for one bag on trips of up to 5 days and for two bags on longer trips.

<!-- pagebreak -->

### 2.2 Hotels

Employees should stay at hotels in the TripDesk preferred list where available. The nightly room rate, excluding taxes, must not exceed the city cap below.

| City tier | Example cities | Nightly cap (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo | 320 |
| Tier 2 | Chicago, Boston, Berlin, Singapore | 240 |
| Tier 3 | All other cities | 180 |

If no room is available under the cap, the employee may book the cheapest available TripDesk option and record the reason in the booking.

### 2.3 Ground Transportation

Taxis and ride-share services are reimbursable for travel between airports, hotels and business meetings. Rental cars are allowed when they are cheaper than taxis or when public transport is not available. Electric or hybrid rental cars should be chosen when available.

Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at 67 cents per mile.

## 3. Meals and Per Diem

Employees traveling overnight receive a daily meal allowance (per diem) instead of submitting meal receipts. The per diem for domestic travel is 75 USD per day and for international travel 95 USD per day. On the first and last day of travel, 75% of the per diem is paid.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client entertainment events approved in advance by a director.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Submitting Expense Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the ExpenseHub system within 30 days of the end of the trip. Late claims require written approval from the finance controller.

Itemized receipts are required for every expense of 25 USD or more.

Approved claims are reimbursed by direct deposit within 7 business days.

### 4.1 Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than three times a year are eligible for a corporate card. Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited and may lead to disciplinary action. Corporate card charges must be reconciled in ExpenseHub within 30 days.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are never reimbursed: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, gym or spa fees, airline seat upgrades purchased for comfort, and travel for spouses or family members.
