# Data Retention and Deletion Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long Helix Systems Inc. keeps different types of records and how records are deleted at the end of their retention period. It supports compliance with the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and tax regulations.

## 2. Retention Schedule

| Record type | Retention period | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer contracts | 7 years after contract end | Legal |
| Invoices and tax records | 10 years | Finance |
| Employee personnel files | 6 years after employment ends | HR |
| Job applicant data | 12 months after the hiring decision | HR |
| Customer support tickets | 3 years after ticket closure | Customer Support |
| Production database backups | 35 days | Engineering |
| Application logs | 90 days | Engineering |
| Security audit logs | 2 years | Security |

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 3. Customer Data After Termination

When a customer terminates a HelixDB Cloud subscription, the customer can export its data for 30 days. After this period, all customer data, including backups, is permanently deleted within a further 60 days. A certificate of deletion is provided on request.

## 4. Data Subject Requests

Requests from individuals to access or delete their personal data must be answered within 30 days. Requests are handled by the privacy team at privacy@helixsystems.example. The identity of the requester must be verified before any data is disclosed.

## 5. Legal Holds

When litigation is expected, the legal team may issue a legal hold. A legal hold suspends deletion of all affected records until the hold is released by the General Counsel, even if the retention period has ended.

## 6. Deletion Methods

Electronic records must be deleted using methods that prevent recovery. Physical documents containing Confidential information must be shredded using cross-cut shredders or placed in the locked secure disposal bins.
