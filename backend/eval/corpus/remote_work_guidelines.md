# Remote Work Guidelines

## Overview

Helix Systems Inc. supports hybrid and remote work. These guidelines describe who can work remotely, what is expected from remote employees and how the company supports them. They complement the Employee Handbook.

## Eligibility

Employees can work remotely after completing their 90-day probation period. Roles that require physical presence, such as data center technicians and office facilities staff, are not eligible for remote work.

Hybrid employees are expected to be in the office at least 2 days per week. Team managers may designate one common office day per week for their team.

## Working From Another Country

Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays require approval from HR and the legal team because of tax and immigration rules. Working from countries under international sanctions is never permitted.

## Home Office Setup

Remote employees receive a monthly internet and phone stipend of 50 USD. The one-time home office allowance described in the Employee Handbook can be used for a desk, chair, monitor or headset.

Employees must make sure that confidential information cannot be seen or heard by other people at home or in public places such as cafes.

## Communication Expectations

Remote employees must be reachable on Slack during core collaboration hours. Cameras should be turned on for team meetings where possible. Messages from teammates should be answered within 4 working hours.

## Coworking Spaces

Employees who live more than 50 miles from a Helix office can expense a coworking space membership of up to 300 USD per month.

## Equipment and Security

Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Company equipment must not be used by family members or friends.
