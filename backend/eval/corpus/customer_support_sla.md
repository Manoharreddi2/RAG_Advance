# Customer Support Service Level Agreement

## Introduction

This document defines the support service levels that Helix Systems Inc. provides to HelixDB Cloud and HelixDB Enterprise customers. Service levels depend on the support plan purchased by the customer: Standard, Premium or Mission Critical.

## Support Plans

| Plan | Support hours | Channels | Named technical account manager |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Business hours, Monday to Friday | Email, web portal | No |
| Premium | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | Email, web portal, phone | No |
| Mission Critical | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | Email, web portal, phone, dedicated Slack channel | Yes |

## Priority Definitions

Priority 1 (P1) means that a production system is down or severely impaired with no workaround. Priority 2 (P2) means that a production system is impaired but a workaround exists. Priority 3 (P3) is a non-production issue or a general question. Priority 4 (P4) is a feature request.

## Initial Response Times

| Priority | Standard | Premium | Mission Critical |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 4 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| P2 | 8 business hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P3 | 2 business days | 1 business day | 4 hours |
| P4 | 5 business days | 3 business days | 2 business days |

## Availability Commitment

HelixDB Cloud has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.95% for Premium and Mission Critical customers and 99.9% for Standard customers.

## Service Credits

If the monthly uptime is below the commitment, customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee if uptime is below the commitment but at least 99.0%, and 25% of the monthly fee if uptime is below 99.0%. Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Service credits are the customer's only remedy for missed availability commitments.

## Escalation

Customers can escalate a ticket by asking for a duty manager. For Mission Critical customers, unresolved P1 tickets are automatically escalated to the Director of Support after 2 hours.

## Exclusions

The service levels do not apply to outages caused by the customer's own applications, scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 days in advance, or force majeure events.
